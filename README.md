## State of the Mod

The randomization for this implementation includes character unlocks and nuggets for unlocking traits and items. Locations are currently limited to Big Quests and Achievements.

## Future Development

This mod will look to fully randomize traits and items in the future, removing the need for nuggets. However, future development is on hold while I work on the How to Fish Archipelago Mod. Feel free to join the Discord and leave some ideas for the direction you'd like the mod to go. I'm always open to fresh ideas!
